# Welcome to my (Emiliano Calvo Alcañiz's) Github page!
I'm a 4th year PhD candidate in the  Entomology and Insect Science GIDP at the University of Arizona in Tucson, under the advisement of Dr. <a href="https://www.annadornhaus.net/"> Anna Dornhaus</a>

My dissertation project is on the role that behavior plays in determining the outcome of a contest (ie. Can a physically weaker individual alter their behavior to win a fight against a stronger opponent?)

To answer this, I'm conducting contests between the ant <i>Temnothorax rugatulus</i>:

🐜Individual and colony size

🐜Previous experience

🐜Aggression

🐜Place of origin
